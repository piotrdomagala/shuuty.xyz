# DM Sans font notice

The local store-listing studio embeds these DM Sans files copied from the
Shuuty product repository:

- `DMSans-Regular.ttf` (400)
- `DMSans-Medium.ttf` (500)
- `DMSans-SemiBold.ttf` (600)
- `DMSans-Bold.ttf` (700)

are not fetched from a CDN.
