# Creative brief: Złoty impuls / Golden Relay

Direction status: selected and produced for the 2026 store refresh.

## Product promise

Shuuty turns a spoken thought into an actionable task that can be delegated immediately. The person, group, due time, reminder, location, status, and task conversation stay together.

Brand shorthand:

- PL: `Powiedz. Deleguj. Działajcie.`
- EN: `Say it. Delegate it. Get it done.`

The opening sequence is fixed:

1. Voice input.
2. Named assignee or group context.
3. Delegated task with visible status and discussion.

Calendar, booking, plans, projects, or time tracking must not appear as the direct result of the voice instruction. They broaden the product story only after the task loop is clear.

## Product breadth

The listing reveals product depth progressively:

1. Advanced voice task creation and delegation.
2. Task ownership, deadlines, reminders, location, and discussion.
3. Groups for work, communities, client coordination, services, sales, and product presentation.
4. Rich descriptions, galleries, public locations, and interests.
5. Group settings, permissions, modules, calendars, bookings, plans, projects, and time tracking.
6. Nearby discovery of meetings, public groups, and offers.

## Flexible groups

A Shuuty group is a configurable space built around a purpose. It is not only a chat.

Credible roles include:

- a workspace for a team;
- a community around an interest;
- a shared context for work with a client;
- a service offer with details, gallery, availability, and conversation;
- a product or sales presentation that encourages a person to ask, join, or book where the release build supports that action.

Do not imply checkout, payments, inventory, or any commerce feature that is not present in the submitted build.

## Locale integrity

Polish and English are separate seeded worlds.

- PL UI uses Polish copy, Olek and Nina, Warsaw locations, and Polish interests.
- EN UI uses English copy, Alex and Noah, London locations, and English interests.
- Speech-language chips match the store locale.
- Dates, times, taxonomy, messages, group descriptions, and task data stay in the same locale.
- No visible email, password, keyboard, debug overlay, loader, toast, or private live location is allowed.

## Visual language

- Background: deep navy `#071522`.
- Primary gold: `#FFE5A0`.
- Strong gold: `#F4C95D`.
- Warm ink: `#FFF9EF`.
- Typography: DM Sans.
- One straight, dominant app capture per image.
- No tilted phone collage and no fake device chrome.
- Real UI occupies at least 80% of each phone image.
- One restrained Golden Relay raster connects the stage label to the current gold `S`.
- Headlines stay readable at thumbnail size and use no more than two lines.

## Publication rules

- Every claim must exist in the submitted build.
- Use only fictional demo data and approved images.
- Keep every visible avatar, cover, and gallery image bound to `provenance/media-assets.json`; copyright is owner-attested, while likeness remains `to-be-confirmed` wherever a person is visible.
- Do not expose emails, passwords, home addresses, private live locations, production notifications, or real private messages.
- Do not use `best`, `#1`, awards, prices, unsupported AI claims, or install prompts inside artwork.
- Screenshots, descriptions, privacy declarations, age rating, pricing, and the binary must describe the same release.
- Perform a native iOS parity check before Apple upload when the final iOS build can be opened on a Mac.
- Resolve the current iPad declaration before App Store upload: either validate and supply 13-inch iPad screenshots or return the release target to iPhone-only.
- Confirm the iOS AppIcon and Android adaptive launcher icon use the same approved gold `S` master as the store listing.
- Do not upload, publish, deploy, or merge without explicit approval.
