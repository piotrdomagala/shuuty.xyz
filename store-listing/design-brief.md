# Creative brief: One thought moves forward

## Product promise

Shuuty turns a spoken thought into a ready task that can be delegated immediately
with the person, due time, reminder, and conversation kept in one context.

Brand shorthand:

- PL: `Powiedz. Deleguj. Działajcie.`
- EN: `Say it. Delegate it. Get it done.`

The first three screenshots must prove the promise as one continuous story:

1. The user says what needs doing.
2. Shuuty prepares a structured task.
3. The task is delegated immediately and its state stays visible.

Planner, task conversation, groups, meetings, and nearby discovery extend this
story. They must not compete with the voice-to-task promise in the opening set.

## Visual language

- Use the current Shuuty tokens: warm white `#FFFBF5`, ink `#1C1917`, deep navy
  `#0D1117`, gold `#FFE5A0`, peach `#FFB088`, and blue `#7DD3FC`.
- Use DM Sans and the typography hierarchy already present in the app.
- Real UI must occupy at least 80% of every screenshot.
- Keep each headline to one benefit, at most two lines.
- Show one large, straight screen crop. Do not use tilted phone collages, heavy
  device chrome, fake status bars, or repeated app logos.
- A restrained gold thread may connect the opening sequence: voice waveform →
  parsed task → delegated status → planner timeline.
- Keep the UI, people, task, dates, and group context consistent across the set.
- Prefer readable light-theme product captures for the core story. One optional
  dark-theme treatment may be explored as an A/B variant, not mixed randomly.

## Google Play feature graphic

Create one localisation-independent `1024×500` master. The central motif is a
gold voice waveform resolving into a task check and a subtle status trail on a
deep navy field. Do not repeat the full app icon, add store badges, or rely on
copy that needs translation. Keep all essential detail inside the central safe
area because Google crops this asset on some surfaces.

## Preview video

Target 24 seconds and show only the current release UI:

1. 0–3 s: spoken instruction.
2. 3–7 s: parsed person, due time, and reminder.
3. 7–10 s: immediate delegation.
4. 10–14 s: task state in Planner.
5. 14–18 s: conversation and group context.
6. 18–21 s: meeting and nearby discovery.
7. 21–24 s: black field, gold `S`, and the localised brand shorthand.

The video must remain understandable without sound. At least 80% of its runtime
should show the real application. Produce a 15–30 second Apple H.264 export and
an embeddable, unlisted YouTube version for Google Play.

## Hard rules

- Every claim must exist in the submitted build.
- Use only fictional demo data and approved imagery.
- Do not expose email addresses, private messages, home addresses, precise
  private locations, real notification content, or real profile photos.
- Do not show Android UI on App Store assets or iOS UI on Google Play assets.
- Do not use `best`, `#1`, awards, prices, install calls to action, or unsupported
  AI claims.
- Screenshots, descriptions, privacy declarations, age rating, and the binary
  must describe the same product.
